# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript chosen for a fast, maintainable portfolio that deploys cleanly to Vercel and does not need an application framework.

## Users

Small and medium business owners in Rio de Janeiro and across Brazil who need a professional digital presence and may arrive through Gustavo's direct outreach.

## Product Purpose

Present Gustavo Martins' web design and development service, make the offer understandable in seconds, and help a prospective client start a conversation through WhatsApp.

## Positioning

Modern websites and landing pages with a strong visual finish and an accessible scope for growing businesses.

## Operating Context

The portfolio link is shared during cold outreach when a lead asks to see examples. Visitors are likely to open it on a phone and decide quickly whether Gustavo's work feels professional and relevant.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One-page portfolio in Brazilian Portuguese.
- Initial services: institutional websites and landing pages.
- Primary contact: WhatsApp.
- Three conceptual project cases are available: AURA (Moda), KŌEN (Gastronomia), and Mateus Valença (Serviços / Advocacia).
- Contact details are temporary and must remain easy to update.
- Deployment target: Vercel, with source control on GitHub.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Gustavo Martins.
- Positioning line: “Sites modernos para negócios que querem crescer no digital.”
- Voice: direct, clear, modern, and approachable.
- Visual direction: light colors with black contrast, refined motion, and a non-generic finish.

## Evidence on Hand

No real client projects, testimonials, or performance numbers are available. AURA, KŌEN, and Mateus Valença are conceptual portfolio projects and must not be presented as paid client work.

## Product Principles

- Make the service understandable before asking for attention.
- Use visual craft as evidence of capability.
- Keep contact immediate and low-friction.
- Never fabricate clients, results, or testimonials.
- Keep the portfolio easy to extend with future cases.
