# Home surface brief

## Scope and mode

Single-page portfolio. Persuade: help a prospective client understand the offer, trust the visual quality, and start a WhatsApp conversation.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A website should feel like forward motion made visible. The page refuses the familiar centered freelancer hero followed by a grid of identical service cards.

**OWN-WORLD:** Near-white paper, dense black fields, cool pale blue, sharp rules, oversized kinetic typography, compact functional labels, and square-to-soft geometry. Motion behaves like a well-tuned typesetting machine: controlled, directional, and never ornamental noise.

**STORY:** Gustavo turns a business idea into a clear, modern digital presence. The visitor first understands what he makes, then sees the two focused offers, learns the short working process, and reaches a direct WhatsApp invitation. No client proof is invented; the future cases area is clearly reserved for the next phase.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A split composition fills the screen. The offer and primary action occupy the left. A black kinetic type field on the right cycles through “SITE”, “LANDING”, “PRESENÇA”, and “MOVIMENTO”; pointer movement shifts its rows with measured parallax. The service is understood before the first scroll, and the WhatsApp action remains visible.

**FORM:** Code-led, typographic studio index. The memorable interaction is the kinetic type field responding to pointer position and then resolving into a calm composition when the pointer leaves. The unavailable Impeccable engine prevented a seed key from being generated; this direction follows the user's pinned light/black, modern, animated brief directly.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
